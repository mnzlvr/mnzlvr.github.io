My creative journey started early, studying photographic laboratory work and spending my youth immersed in the world of analog photography. Over time, this path shifted toward music, where I spent nearly two decades performing with several bands and DJing for more than ten years — including a residency in London.

A couple of years ago, I picked up both digital and analog cameras again and returned to photography with renewed clarity. Today, I merge the two worlds that shaped me: music and visual storytelling. My focus lies in capturing live shows, artists, and the raw energy surrounding them.
