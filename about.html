About

Music + photography entwined — early darkroom training, 20 years in music (bands & DJ residencies), now capturing live shows and artist portraits.

Oliver Muñoz

Oliver studied photographic laboratory work early on and spent his youth immersed in analogue photography. Later he transitioned into the music world, playing with several bands and DJing for over a decade, including a long residency in London.

A couple of years ago he returned to photography with both digital and analog cameras. Today Oliver combines two lifelong passions — music and visual storytelling — focusing on live shows, artist promotion shots and capturing the atmosphere that connects both worlds.
