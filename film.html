Film

There's a texture and permanence to film that digital can't replicate. My preferred medium is 35mm, often using Ilford HP5 and Delta 3200 for their iconic grain structure and dynamic range. Working with film demands a slower, more intentional process—each click matters.

This series is a collection of street, portrait, and documentary moments captured primarily on a Canon AE-1, preserving moments in their true, timeless form.
